### Will paperlight slow down my computer?

No. paperlight is designed to be lightweight. It runs quietly in the background and only uses resources when you're searching.

### Does my computer need to be powerful?

No, paperlight works well on almost any Windows computer, including older machines. It's optimized for speed and efficiency.

### Can I search for files on external drives or network folders?

paperlight focuses on your PC's local documents. For best results, keep your files in standard locations like your Documents folder or Desktop.

Make sure the file is in a location paperlight indexes. If you just added new files, give it a few minutes to update its index. You can also try searching with a different keyword or phrase.

Absolutely. paperlight operates entirely on your computer. No data leaves your machine. It's read-only, meaning it cannot alter or damage your files.
